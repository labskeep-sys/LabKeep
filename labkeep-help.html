Help Centre

How Can We Help?

Find answers to some of the most common questions about using LabKeep to find laboratory supplies, connect with suppliers and biomedical engineers, and explore inventory management.

How do I find laboratory supplies?

Go to the Supplies section to browse laboratory products by category. You can also use the search bar to search for specific products, suppliers, engineers or services.

How do I make an enquiry?

When you find a product you are interested in, select the enquiry or request option on the product. You can then use WhatsApp to send your enquiry to LabKeep. You can include the quantity you need and any other information that may help with your request.

How do I contact a supplier?

Visit the Suppliers section to explore suppliers listed on LabKeep. Supplier profiles provide information about the products and services they offer.

How do I find a biomedical engineer?

Visit the Engineers section to explore biomedical engineers and their areas of technical expertise. You can review their profiles and contact them based on the services you need.

How does inventory management work?

LabKeep's inventory management system is designed to help laboratories keep track of their laboratory supplies, quantities, stock levels and important inventory information in one place. Visit the Inventory section to learn more.

How do I join LabKeep as a supplier?

Visit the Suppliers page and select the option to join the LabKeep supplier network. You will be able to provide information about your business, products and service locations.

How do I join as a biomedical engineer?

Visit the Engineers page and select the option to join the LabKeep biomedical engineer network. You will be able to provide your professional information, areas of expertise and services.

How do I contact LabKeep?

You can contact LabKeep directly through WhatsApp using the contact option below. For enquiries about supplies, suppliers, biomedical engineers or inventory management, please include enough information for us to understand how we can assist you.

Still Need Help?

If you couldn't find the answer you were looking for, chat with LabKeep directly on WhatsApp and we'll be happy to assist you.
