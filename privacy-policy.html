Privacy Policy

Your Information and Privacy

This Privacy Policy explains, in general terms, how LabKeep may handle information provided through its website, enquiries, supplier applications, biomedical engineer applications and inventory-related services.

Last updated: September 2026

1. About This Privacy Policy

LabKeep is a digital platform intended to connect diagnostic laboratories with laboratory suppliers, biomedical engineers and inventory management solutions.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be provided to LabKeep and the general purposes for which that information may be used.

As LabKeep develops, its services, technology and data processing activities may change. This Privacy Policy may therefore be updated from time to time to reflect those changes.

2. Information You May Provide

Depending on how you use LabKeep, you may choose to provide information such as:

  • Name or contact person's name
  • Phone number or WhatsApp contact details
  • Email address
  • Business or organisation information
  • Business or service location
  • Information about products or services offered
  • Professional information provided by biomedical engineers
  • Information included in enquiries or messages
  • Information relating to laboratory inventory, where the inventory service is used

You should avoid submitting information that is not necessary for the purpose of your enquiry or application.

3. Enquiries

When you contact LabKeep about laboratory supplies, suppliers, biomedical engineers, inventory management or another service, the information you provide may be used to understand and respond to your enquiry.

Where an enquiry relates to a supplier, engineer or other service provider, relevant information may need to be shared with the appropriate party in order to respond to the enquiry or facilitate the requested connection.

4. Supplier Applications

If you apply to join the LabKeep supplier network, you may provide information about yourself, your business, products, location, contact details and services.

This information may be used to review the application, communicate with the applicant and, where applicable, support the supplier's listing or participation on LabKeep.

Submission of an application does not automatically guarantee acceptance or publication on the LabKeep platform.

5. Biomedical Engineer Applications

If you apply to join LabKeep as a biomedical engineer, you may provide professional and contact information, including information about your experience, qualifications, areas of expertise and services.

This information may be used to review the application, communicate with the applicant and, where applicable, support the engineer's profile or participation on LabKeep.

Applicants should only provide information that is relevant to their professional application.

6. Inventory-Related Information

LabKeep may provide inventory management features intended to help laboratories organise and manage information about their laboratory supplies and stock.

The type of information collected or stored through such features may depend on the inventory tools and functionality available at the time the service is used.

Users should avoid entering personal information that is not necessary for inventory management. Users are also responsible for ensuring that they have the appropriate authority to enter information belonging to their organisation or other people.

7. How Information May Be Used

Information provided to LabKeep may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to enquiries and requests
  • Reviewing supplier and engineer applications
  • Communicating with users about LabKeep services
  • Connecting users with relevant suppliers or engineers where appropriate
  • Providing and improving LabKeep services
  • Maintaining the security and functionality of the platform
  • Meeting applicable legal or regulatory requirements

8. Sharing of Information

LabKeep does not intend to make personal information publicly available simply because it has been submitted through an enquiry or application.

However, information may be shared where this is necessary to respond to an enquiry, process an application, provide a requested service, operate the platform through service providers, or comply with a legal or regulatory obligation.

Where supplier or engineer profile information is intended to be displayed publicly, the information made public should be limited to information appropriate for that purpose.

9. Third-Party Services

LabKeep may use third-party services to support parts of its website or operations. Examples may include website hosting, communication tools, forms, analytics or other technical services.

The information processed by those services may be subject to the relevant third party's own privacy terms and policies.

As LabKeep's technology develops, the third-party services it uses may also change.

10. Data Security

LabKeep intends to take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the information and the services being provided to help protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, individuals may have rights relating to their personal information, including rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing activities.

Requests relating to personal information can be directed to LabKeep using the contact details provided on the Contact Us page.

12. Children's Information

LabKeep's services are intended for laboratories, organisations, suppliers, biomedical engineers and other professional users. LabKeep does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through its services.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

LabKeep may update this Privacy Policy when its services, technology, data processing practices or applicable requirements change.

The updated version will be made available on this page, with the date of the latest update shown near the beginning of the policy.
