About LabKeep

Keeping Laboratories Ready, Always.

LabKeep is a digital platform that connects diagnostic laboratories with trusted suppliers, biomedical engineers and smart inventory solutions, all in one place.

Who We Are

Laboratories depend on reliable supplies, functional equipment, technical support and effective inventory management to provide quality diagnostic services.

LabKeep was created to make it easier for laboratories to find these essential resources by bringing suppliers, biomedical engineers and laboratory inventory solutions together on one platform.

Our goal is to simplify the process of finding what a laboratory needs and connecting with the right people to keep laboratory operations running.

One Connected Laboratory Ecosystem

From laboratory supplies and technical support to inventory management, LabKeep brings important laboratory resources together so laboratories can spend less time searching and more time focusing on their work.

What LabKeep Does

LabKeep connects different parts of the laboratory support ecosystem through three core areas.

Laboratory Supplies

Discover laboratory products across categories such as reagents and test kits, consumables, equipment, glassware, laboratory furniture and safety products.

Biomedical Engineers

Connect laboratories with biomedical engineers and technical professionals for installation, maintenance, troubleshooting, repairs and other equipment support.

Smart Inventory

Help laboratories keep track of stock, monitor expiry dates and identify products that may need to be reordered.

Our Mission & Vision

Building a more connected and prepared laboratory ecosystem.

Our Mission

To make laboratory procurement, technical support and inventory management more connected, accessible and convenient for diagnostic laboratories.

Our Vision

To contribute to a stronger laboratory ecosystem where laboratories can readily find the supplies, technical expertise and tools they need to stay prepared.
