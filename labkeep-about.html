About LabKeep

LabKeep is a digital platform designed to connect diagnostic laboratories with the supplies, trusted suppliers, biomedical engineers and inventory solutions they need to keep their laboratories ready.

Who We Are

Laboratories need reliable access to supplies, technical support and effective inventory management to operate efficiently. LabKeep brings these essential resources together in one connected platform, making it easier for laboratories to find what they need and connect with the right people.

Laboratory Supplies

Find laboratory supplies from different categories, including reagents and test kits, consumables, equipment, glassware, furniture and safety products.

Biomedical Engineers

Connect with biomedical engineers and technical professionals who provide services such as installation, maintenance, troubleshooting and equipment support.

Inventory Management

Support better laboratory stock management by helping laboratories keep track of supplies, monitor expiry dates and identify when products may need to be reordered.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make laboratory procurement, technical support and inventory management more connected and accessible.


By bringing laboratories, suppliers and biomedical engineers together, LabKeep aims to help laboratories spend less time searching for what they need and more time focusing on quality diagnostic services.

Keeping Labs Ready, Always.

From finding laboratory supplies to connecting with technical professionals and managing laboratory stock, LabKeep is built around one simple goal: helping laboratories stay prepared.
